## N01 · Binaural beats to entrain the brain? A systematic review of the effects of binaural beat stimulation on brain oscillatory activity, and the implications for psychological research and intervention

Ruth Maria Ingendoh; Ella S. Posny; Angela Heine. 2023-05-19.

[Source / publication record](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0286023)

**Access in original review:** Full article consulted

**Design / source type:** Systematic review of 14 EEG studies

**Relevant finding:** 5 supportive, 8 negative, 1 mixed; heterogeneous methods prevent a settled entrainment conclusion.

**Limits:** Small samples; inconsistent operationalization and analysis; no full Gateway curriculum test.

## N02 · Efficacy of binaural auditory beats in cognition, anxiety, and pain perception: a meta-analysis

Miguel Garcia-Argibay; Miguel A. Santed; Jose M. Reales. 2019-03.

[Source / publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30073406/)

**Access in original review:** NLM abstract consulted; full text not reviewed

**Design / source type:** 22 studies; 35 effect sizes

**Relevant finding:** Overall Hedges' g=0.45 for pooled cognitive, anxiety, and pain outcomes.

**Limits:** Mixed outcomes; pooled result is not proof of mechanism or Gateway-wide efficacy.

## N03 · Efficacy of theta binaural beat therapy on pain, cognition and anxiety in adults: A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials

Iram Fatima; Sahar Zaidi; Habiba Sundus; Md Farhan Alam; Huma Parveen; Sohrab Ahmad Khan. 2026-09/2026-10.

[Source / publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42349368/) · [Alternate access](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S155083072600159X)
