# Research Roadmap

[Home](../README.md) · [Methodology](METHODOLOGY.md) · [Sources](SOURCES.md) · [Contribute](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

**About 7 minutes.** These are open research questions, not promised findings or unfinished reader resources.

The 1983 memorandum becomes most useful when its explanations are converted back into questions.

## Eight core questions

1. **Does Hemi-Sync outperform matched controls?**<br>
   Compare it with matched music, matched verbal suggestion, neutral audio, and silence on preregistered outcomes.

2. **Who responds, and why?**<br>
   Test expectancy, hypnotic suggestibility, prior meditation, sleep, personality/absorption, and individual auditory response as moderators.

3. **Are Focus states physiologically reproducible?**<br>
   Do claimed Focus 10/12/15/21 states correspond to reliable EEG, autonomic, sleep-stage, behavioral, or phenomenological patterns across people and sessions?

4. **Do dramatic sessions predict useful outcomes?**<br>
   Compare subjective “depth” with next-day and long-term outcomes such as sleep, concentration, stress, functioning, and behavior.

5. **Can OBE reports identify concealed targets?**<br>
   Use preregistered targets, blinding, locked reports, independent judges, decoys, and complete reporting.

6. **Can remote-viewing effects independently replicate?**<br>
   Change viewers, targets, experimenters, judges, laboratories, and analysis teams.

7. **What is the proposed physical coupling?**<br>
   If physiology is said to couple to an external field, specify the field, units, source strength, coupling coefficient, information encoding, receiver, noise, and predicted effect size.

8. **What survives neutralization of the metaphysics?**<br>
   Compare traditional Gateway language with neutral instructions matched for relaxation, attention, expectancy, imagery, and session structure.

## Priority research tracks

### Track A — Direct Gateway/Hemi-Sync trials

**High value because:** most adjacent evidence is about binaural beats, meditation, hypnosis, or generic audio rather than the full Gateway protocol.

Useful study features:

- active controls,
- expectancy measurement,
- preregistered primary outcomes,
- adequate sample size,
- repeated sessions,
- physiological + behavioral + subjective outcomes,
- public analysis plan and materials where possible.

### Track B — Focus-state characterization

The phrase “Focus 10” is meaningful inside the Monroe/Gateway system, but the stronger scientific claim is that it maps to a reproducible state.

A useful protocol would combine:

- polysomnography or EEG,
- heart rate / HRV,
- respiration,
- eye movements,
- behavioral probes,
- structured phenomenology,
- within-person replication.

### Track C — Self-location and OBE

Modern work on body ownership, vestibular integration, temporoparietal regions, virtual reality, sleep transitions, and lucid dreaming gives testable alternatives to literal disembodiment.

The strongest next step is not arguing about metaphysics; it is asking whether a reported OBE carries **veridical concealed information**.

### Track D — Remote viewing replication

A confirmatory design should be harder to explain through:

- sensory leakage,
- target cueing,
- judge effects,
- flexible scoring,
- repeated-target familiarity,
- optional stopping,
- selective reporting,
- shared staff or procedures across “replications.”

The ideal result would survive a new lab and independent analysis.

### Track E — Historical source archaeology

Open provenance questions include:

- the archival path of specific copies,
- why printed page 25 is absent from the government-preserved facsimile,
- original Monroe Institute materials cited by McDonnell,
- the exact status and availability of Menninger/Twemlow/Jager materials referenced in the memo,
- contemporaneous Army training context.

Important rule: **absence of a page is a provenance question before it is a conspiracy claim.**

### Track F — Reassessing the original bibliography

The 1983 bibliography is itself a research object.

A useful project would classify every cited work as:

- primary experiment,
- theoretical paper,
- review,
- popular-science synthesis,
- spiritual/philosophical work,
- first-person report,
- institute/training material.

Then compare what McDonnell says each source establishes with what the source actually establishes.

### Track G — Philosophy of mind

Keep empirical and philosophical questions separate while studying:

- physicalism,
- dualism,
- idealism,
- panpsychism,
- neutral monism,
- theories of self,
- epistemology of mystical experience.

Philosophy can clarify what is coherent or conceivable. It cannot turn an unsupported empirical mechanism into a measured one.

---

## What would materially change the current assessment?

Evidence strong enough to move the project should include one or more of:

- preregistered direct Gateway trials with matched controls and robust outcomes,
- replicated physiology specific to claimed Focus states,
- independently replicated concealed-target success with strong blinding,
- a physically specified and experimentally confirmed external coupling mechanism,
- operational remote-viewing accuracy that can be identified prospectively rather than after feedback,
- high-quality negative replications that bound or eliminate previously reported effects.

## Null results can still be valuable

A failed paranormal prediction can coexist with:

- improved relaxation,
- useful imagery,
- better sleep,
- a reproducible physiological state,
- personal meaning.

Likewise, a positive subjective result does not need to imply a paranormal mechanism.

Good research lets these outcomes separate.
