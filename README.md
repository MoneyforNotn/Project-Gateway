# Gateway Process — Evidence Review & Source Archive

How far can an image reach beyond the person who holds it? A 1983 Army memorandum approached that question through sound, altered states, and a universal hologram. Project Gateway preserves the record, examines the evidence, and offers a six-week course that reads the memorandum alongside Charles F. Haanel’s *The Master Key System*.

## What is this document?

Lt. Col. Wayne M. McDonnell submitted *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process* on 9 June 1983 within the U.S. Army Intelligence and Security Command. It attempts to explain the Monroe Institute’s Gateway Experience. The CIA later held and released a copy. Declassification made the record public. The strength of each explanation depends on its own evidence. [Government catalog](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943) · [Editorial notes](research/PROVENANCE_AND_EDITORIAL_NOTES.md)

## What would count as a result?

An image might change how a situation feels or suggest a different next move. Discovering a concealed fact and directly influencing an event are two other claims, each with its own test. Haanel and McDonnell also ask what mind is. Their philosophical arguments give us questions to think through alongside the empirical ones. [Evidence map](research/EVIDENCE_AT_A_GLANCE.md#four-questions-to-keep-separate) · [Methodology](research/METHODOLOGY.md)
