McDonnell, Wayne M. *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process.* Department of the Army, U.S. Army Intelligence and Security Command, 9 June 1983. CIA Reading Room identifier CIA-RDP96-00788R001700210016-5.

Use the [GovInfo catalog](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/GOVPUB-D101-PURL-gpo152943) and [preserved facsimile](source/01_Source_GovInfo_CIA_Release.pdf). The [historical CIA PDF URL](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/cia-rdp96-00788r001700210016-5.pdf) may redirect to maintenance.

When citing printed page 25, identify the [supplementary complete copy](source/02_Supplement_Complete_Copy_With_Page_25.pdf). That page is absent from the government-preserved 29-leaf copy. See the [editorial policy](research/PROVENANCE_AND_EDITORIAL_NOTES.md).
